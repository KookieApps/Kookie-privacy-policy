02 Frequently asked questions

Can I transfer my data to a new phone?

Kookie stores all data locally on your device. You can transfer it via an iCloud backup (iOS Settings → Your name → iCloud → Backup). Kookie has no cloud sync of its own — your data always stays on your phone.

URL recipe import isn't working — what should I do?

Make sure you have an internet connection. Some websites block automated fetching — try adding the recipe manually using the "Create recipe" option instead.

How do I turn off analytics?

In the app: Settings → Privacy → Anonymous analytics. The change takes effect immediately.
